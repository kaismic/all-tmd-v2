\section{NOR-TMD and Sydney Dataset Controlled Comparison}

This experiment is a controlled comparison of NOR-TMD-only, Sydney-only, and pooled NOR-TMD + Sydney training using seven Leave-one-out cross-validation (LOOCV) folds. The controlled stage groups all Sydney recordings by participant. Experimental configurations were copied from preliminary experiments and remained identical unless otherwise specified.

\subsection{Methodology}

Before the controlled Sydney evaluation, XGBoost hyperparameters were selected using 45 Optuna trials on NOR-TMD alone. Selection used five participant-grouped folds, seed 42, and macro F1 calculated from pooled out-of-fold predictions; no Sydney observations entered this search. The selected hyperparameters were then fixed across all training conditions, calibration fractions, and study seeds. For each condition, a new XGBoost model was fitted on the specified training rows; no fitted model from source tuning was reused in the Sydney-only or pooled conditions.

This source-only selection provided a data-driven common configuration while keeping the Sydney evaluation outcomes out of hyperparameter tuning. NOR-TMD tuning was a design choice rather than a requirement for obtaining initial hyperparameter values: default or prespecified values could also have been fixed before evaluation. Because the common values were selected on NOR-TMD, the comparison estimates performance under a source-selected configuration and does not establish the performance of separately tuned Sydney-only or pooled models.

For each of seven outer folds, every usable window belonging to one Sydney participant is reserved for testing. Sydney calibration is drawn only from the other six participants. NOR-TMD-only training uses no Sydney rows; Sydney-only training uses the selected local sessions; pooled training uses NOR-TMD plus exactly the same local sessions.

Every participant is tested once for each combination of training source, calibration fraction, and seed, and the seven prediction sets are pooled before computing the aggregate metrics.

There are three NOR-TMD-only evaluations (seeds 42, 43, and 44) and 30 Sydney-containing evaluations (two conditions $\times$ five fractions $\times$ three seeds), giving 33 evaluations and 231 recorded fold evaluations (33 evaluations $\times$ seven participants). The seed numbers are arbitrary numbers used to initialize a pseudo-random state to control which Sydney sessions enter each class-balanced calibration subset and the XGBoost model’s initial randomized state.

Within each fold and class, sessions are placed in a deterministic order for each seed. For a requested fraction $f<1$, the prefix length is $\max(1,\lfloor f n_c\rfloor)$, where $n_c$ is the number of eligible training-pool sessions in class $c$. At $f=1$, all training-pool sessions are selected. The 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1 subsets are nested and paired between Sydney-only and pooled training. Rounding and unequal session lengths change the effective fractions (Table~\ref{tab:transfer-effective}).
